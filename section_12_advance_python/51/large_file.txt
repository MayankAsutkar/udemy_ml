India elects tribal candidate Droupadi Murmu as president ...Smt. Droupadi Murmu is the 15th and current President of India, having assumed office on July 25, 2022. She is the head of state, commander-in-chief of the Indian Armed Forces, and the first person belonging to a tribal community to hold the position. Previously, she served as the Governor of Jharkhand.

Key details about the President of India:
Current President: Smt. Droupadi Murmu (since July 25, 2022).
Background: Born on June 20, 1958, in Odisha, she has a background in education and served as the Governor of Jharkhand (2015–2021).
Role: The President is the ceremonial head of state of India, with executive powers exercised mainly on the advice of the Prime Minister and the Cabinet.
Term: The President is elected for a five-year term.
Residence: The official residence is the Rashtrapati Bhavan in New Delhi.
